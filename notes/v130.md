**Isekai Village v130**: Roomier Phone Windows

📱 **Roomier phone windows**: on a phone, an open window now gets the whole screen with one compact header. The town's top bar and toolbar step aside while you're in it and come straight back when you close it.

🔍 **Larger interface sizes fit**: at 115% to 150% a window now starts right under the top bar (it used to sit far too low), so far more of every activity fits on screen, even on short phones.

📖 **Story scenes wait for you**: a story card pauses on the same line while a phone window is open and carries on when you close it.

✨ **Also new since v128**: everything in v129 (raids that test real strength, Soul Awakening, the Royal Treasury, nine new buildings, calmer windows, and the Hall of Living Relics fix). See the v129 release below for the full list.

🍎 **Mac and 📱 iPhone/iPad**: the apps tell you when a new version is out and open this page. Download each new version from here.
